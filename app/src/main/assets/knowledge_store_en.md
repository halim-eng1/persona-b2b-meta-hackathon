## Shipping & Delivery
- **Cairo and Giza**: 60 EGP, delivered within 24 to 48 hours.
- **Alexandria, Delta, and Canal cities**: 85 EGP, delivered within 2 to 3 business days.
- **Upper Egypt, Matrouh, and Red Sea**: 120 EGP, delivered within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Free shipping**: Orders above 3,000 EGP qualify for free delivery nationwide.
- **Order tracking**: A tracking link is sent by WhatsApp as soon as the order leaves the warehouse.
- **Delivery window**: Couriers call before arrival; if the customer is unavailable, the order is rescheduled once at no extra cost.

---

## Exchange, Returns & Refunds
- Customers may exchange or return unused items within **14 days** of delivery.
- Items must be in their original condition, with tags and original packaging intact.
- Made-to-order and personalized items are final sale and cannot be returned.
- If the item arrived damaged or incorrect, Atlas & Oak covers all shipping costs both ways.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after inspection.
- To start a return, send the order number and a photo of the item on WhatsApp to +20 100 555 0199.

## Payment Methods
- **Cash on delivery** for orders up to 5,000 EGP.
- **Credit and debit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, Meeza) through the secure checkout.
- **Mobile wallets** (Vodafone Cash, InstaPay) — send the receipt on WhatsApp to confirm.
- **Bank transfer** for corporate and bulk orders; the order ships after the transfer clears.
- Invoices are issued automatically for every order and can be requested in PDF format.

## Orders, Bulk & Corporate Gifting
- Minimum order quantity for corporate gifting is 10 units per product.
- Bulk orders receive a 10% discount above 20 units and a 15% discount above 50 units.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Do you ship outside Egypt?** Not yet; international shipping is planned for next year.
- **Can I change my order after placing it?** Yes, within 2 hours of ordering, as long as it has not shipped.
- **Are the wood products sealed?** Yes, they are finished with food-safe oil and should be re-oiled every 3 to 6 months.
- **Do you offer installation?** The floor lamp is assembly-free; the team can advise by video call if needed.
- **Is there a physical store to see products?** Yes, the Zamalek showroom displays the full catalog.
