Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

Q: How do I track my package?
A: Once your order is shipped, you will receive an email and WhatsApp confirmation with your courier tracking link. You can also track your order status directly on our website at https://store.example.com/track.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and Cash on Delivery (COD) in eligible delivery zones.

Q: How do I care for my organic cotton garments?
A: For best longevity, machine wash cold (30°C / 85°F) with like colors inside out. Hang dry or tumble dry low. Do not bleach or dry clean.
